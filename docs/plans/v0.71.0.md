# meld v0.71.0 plan (draft)

This is work in progress. Nothing here is tagged or claimed. The scope is drafted in rivet as SR-134 to SR-139, at `status: draft` and `release: v0.71.0`. Each item names the North Star objective it serves (pulseengine.eu `data/north-star.toml`), because `serves:` does not exist in the schema yet.

The plan was drafted by one Fable agent and checked by a second Fable agent that saw only `north-star.toml` and the draft. The revisions below come from that check.

## Baseline (main at 370d82b)

- **Canonical ABI differential** (640 rows: 320 fixtures under `--memory multi` and `--memory shared`):

  | match | match-trap | refused | accepts-invalid | wrong | fused-trap | not-runnable |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | 205 | 59 | 316 (124 multi, 192 shared) | 30 | 20 | 8 | 2 |

- **Upstream component-model WAST, exec baseline:** 36 pass and 9 no-trap. 266 commands need host imports the harness lacks, 270 sit in components meld does not fuse, 79 have no export, and 44 are unsupported.
- **Fuse baseline:** 182 fused and 152 refused. 6 are meld-invalid (meld wrote an invalid module).

## What the indicators say

The check found that no bet moves an O1, O3 or O5 indicator as `north-star.toml` defines it:

| Objective | The indicator counts | Status for these bets |
|---|---|---|
| O3 | proof-carrying repos with a required proof check | a differential ledger is not that |
| O5 | tools and CI gates running as signed components under kiln, with wasmtime as the differential | not met while kiln cannot run them |
| O1 | runs on hardware | none of these bets runs on hardware |

So each bet below says which objective's *summary* it serves and leaves that indicator unmoved. No bet serves the committed 2026-Q4 O2 target.

## Committed

### Bet 1: zero silent wrong answers, and a re-check kit (SR-134)

- **Serves:** the O3 summary (every verified claim is backed); the indicator is unmoved.
- **Target:** every `wrong` and `accepts-invalid` row moves to match, match-trap or a loud refusal: wrong 20 → 0, accepts-invalid 30 → 0. #622 is settled as a fix or as a documented, tested rule.
- **Deliberate fused-trap rows:** a capped list of at most 8, which may only shrink.
- **Re-check kit:** a release asset holding the fixtures, the ledger and one script that re-derives every row with the signed binary and stock wasmtime.
- **Acceptance:** a per-PR job fails on any `wrong` or `accepts-invalid` row, any row leaving `match`, or a fused-trap list longer than its cap.
- **Negative controls:**
  - The kit must report drift against the released v0.69.0 binary.
  - A planted wrong ledger row must fail the job.
- **Kill:** if more than half of the 50 rows end `refused` rather than `match` or `match-trap`, report it as a retreat and move the shapes to bet 2b.
- **Dependencies:** wsc signs the kit. None are blocking.

### Bet 2a: every refusal carries a reason (SR-135)

- **Serves:** the O3 summary; the indicator is unmoved.
- **What:** a refusal-reason column in the ledger, filled from the typed error, plus a histogram.
- **Acceptance:** the ledger test fails on any `refused` row without a reason.
- **Negative control:** a planted reason-less row fails the test.
- **Kill:** none needed. This is the cheapest and most useful item, and it is the precondition for bet 2b.

### Bet 3: upstream WAST as the public instrument (SR-136)

- **Serves:** the O3 summary, and it is the strongest fit, because the corpus is external; the indicator is unmoved.
- **What:** a host-import harness so the `imports` commands execute, plus fixes for the 6 meld-invalid and 9 no-trap rows. `cm_wast_ratchet` becomes a required check.
- **Acceptance:**
  - meld-invalid 6 → 0 and no-trap 9 → 0.
  - imports 266 → at most 60 not executed.
  - pass 36 → at least 150.
- **Negative control:** a planted `no-trap` row must fail the check.
- **Kill:** decided at the first measurement after the harness lands. If fewer than 114 additional assertions pass and the misses come from async/P3 semantics, the pass target moves to v0.72 with P3. Meld-invalid 0 and no-trap 0 stay committed either way.
- **Dependencies:** wasmtime only.

## Stretch

### Bet 2b: halve the refusals (SR-137)

- **Serves:** the O1 and O5 summaries indirectly, since kiln and synth receive only what meld fuses; the indicators are unmoved.
- **Driven by:** the bet 2a histogram.
- **Target:** refused 316 → at most 160. Wrong and accepts-invalid must not rise.
- **Kill:** if no single cause accounts for at least 40% of refusals, re-plan from the histogram.
- **Caution:** some refusals are correct. A refusal-count target must not reward accepting unsound shapes.

### Bet 5a: meld as a signed WASI component (SR-138)

- **What:** build meld for wasm32-wasip2, fuse it with native meld, sign it with wsc and publish `meld.wasm` (#539).
- **Acceptance:** on wasmtime, fused meld's `--reproducible` output must be byte-identical to native meld's over the fixture corpus.
- **Negative control:** a one-byte-altered output must fail the job.
- **Does not count for O5:** the indicator requires kiln execution, which is blocked on the kiln WASI gap.
- **Kill:** meld does not build for wasip2 without forking a dependency, or fused meld runs more than 10× slower than native.

### Bet 5b: synth static-data gate on a meld composite (SR-139)

- **Precondition, checked first:** the gale inputs must be usable in public CI.
- **What:** a required job that runs pinned synth's VCR-VER-003 validation on a fused gale composite.
- **Negative control:** the meld 0.62.0 composite (#479) must fail the gate.
- **No O1 claim:** there is no hardware run.

## Informational

### Bet 4: a kiln column in the differential

- **What:** every wasmtime `match` and `match-trap` row also runs on a pinned kiln release.
- **Shrink-only list:** rows kiln cannot instantiate are recorded as `kiln-refused` with the kiln issue number, and the list may only shrink.
- **#297 stays open:** the bet does not close it while kiln#375 is open.
- **Status:** ships informational, because the known kiln 0.7.3 WASI gap probably decides the outcome already.

## Deferred
